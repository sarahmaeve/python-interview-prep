# Evidence Packet 1

The gateway incident lasted fourteen minutes. Seven customer submissions each
produced two remote report jobs; unaffected submissions produced one job.

For every affected submission:

- The caller made one logical submission.
- The caller received a transient transport error before receiving a success.
- The two jobs used the same payload.

No duplicate has been identified outside the incident window. Cleanup and
customer impact are owned by the wider incident team.
