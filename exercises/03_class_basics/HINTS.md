# Hints: Exercise 03 — Bank Account

## Hint 1

Create two accounts and compare both their balances and histories.

## Hint 2

Inspect where transaction history is initialized, what `withdraw()` returns,
and how `transfer()` reacts to that return value.

## Hint 3

Move mutable history to per-instance initialization, return the successful
withdrawal result required by the tests, and stop a transfer when withdrawal
does not succeed.
